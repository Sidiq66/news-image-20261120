One image that sums up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026, drawn as a newspaper front page. The lead: Trump holds off new strikes on Iran until 2027 ("I am not rushing into any decision"). Around it: Europe, meeting in Berlin hours after the Shenzhen summit, agrees a united position for talks with Russia on ending the Ukraine war; nearly 300,000 people are evacuated as Cyclone Akvan makes landfall on India's east coast near Gopalpur; Anthropic's record $81bn IPO closes at $97 a share while the S&P 500 falls 2% to a seven-month low; and COP31 reaches its closing day in Antalya with every mention of fossil fuels dropped from the draft deal. A ticker adds Trump's posthumous pardon of Pete Rose and GTA VI's estimated $1.84bn launch day. Every fact was checked against at least two news sources on the morning of 20 November (Reuters, BBC News, Axios, IMD/PTI/ANI among them), and the day's tempting leftovers — the Shenzhen summit itself, the ICJ's Myanmar verdict, Voyager 1's light-day — were left out on purpose: they are 19 November's news, not the 20th's. The page is drawn programmatically by `build_image.py` (PIL, no photographs, no downloaded assets); `main.ts` checks the picture against the gate's limits and places it at the output path as `entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
